The two comics explain how to connect a camera, read shutter and power/wake counters, use readings to follow commercial-shoot usage, and share a serial-masked report during a used-camera sale. They explicitly state that counts cannot predict remaining shutter life or guarantee camera condition. Unverified model/firmware readings are labeled as reference data.

The artwork uses the official Lumerian character masters in `branding/lumerian_master`. Text, panels and layout are rendered by `tools/render_intro_comic.py` with Pillow and a locally available Korean-capable font. The PNGs are the shareable files; the script and source copy make text revisions reproducible.

The comics describe beta.12 functionality. They are educational material for the unofficial community tool, not a Panasonic endorsement or a guarantee of hardware condition.
